## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & Common Issues

Below are the most frequently reported Cherax issues and their verified solutions:

**Fix:** Ensure the target title is fully loaded before starting the Cherax injection. Navigate to **Settings → Privacy & Security** and whitelist the `/Cherax` directory. Verify you're running the official Cherax build `v2.8.4` — older versions may trigger injection failures.

**Fix:** Your Cherax account session has expired. Run `./cherax --reset-token` from the CLI, then log in again through the official Cherax loader. If the issue persists after `3` attempts, contact the verified Cherax support team on Discord.
